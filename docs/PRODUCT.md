# LUDI ON – Product Definition

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Official

**Owner:** LUDI ON Core Team

**Last Update:** July 2026

---

# Vision

LUDI ON is an AI-powered platform that helps teachers design engaging educational games and interactive learning activities in minutes.

The platform combines pedagogy, artificial intelligence, and modern educational design into a single intuitive workflow.

---

# Mission

Reduce lesson preparation time while increasing the quality, creativity, and pedagogical value of classroom activities.

---

# Target Users

Primary users

- Primary school teachers
- Preschool teachers
- Special education teachers

Future users

- Secondary school teachers
- Educational therapists
- School counselors
- Parents

---

# Core Value Proposition

Teachers describe what they want.

LUDI ON designs the learning experience.

---

# Product Pillars

## Simplicity

Easy to learn.

Fast to use.

---

## Pedagogy

Every activity follows sound educational principles.

---

## Artificial Intelligence

AI supports teachers.

---

## Creativity

Every activity should encourage active learning.

---

## Flexibility

Everything generated can be edited.

---

# Success Criteria

A teacher can create a complete classroom activity in less than 5 minutes.

A standard activity can be configured in under 60 seconds.
