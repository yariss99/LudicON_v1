# Why PRIMA exists

Teaching is one of the most intellectually demanding and socially significant professions.

Every lesson influences not only knowledge, but confidence, curiosity, creativity, and the future of learners.

Teachers deserve technology that respects the complexity of their work.

PRIMA exists to help teachers design meaningful learning experiences while preserving their professional autonomy.

PRIMA is not built to replace teachers.

**PRIMA exists because teachers matter.**

---

# Our belief

Education is not the transmission of information.

Education is the intentional design of learning.

Technology should amplify professional expertise rather than replace it.

Artificial intelligence should reduce routine work while increasing the time available for human interaction, creativity, and reflection.

# The seven foundational principles

## 1 — Teachers remain authors

Every educational decision belongs to the teacher.

AI provides recommendations.  
Teachers make decisions.

## 2 — Pedagogy leads technology

Technology is valuable only when it improves learning.

Every feature must answer one question:

> How does this improve learning?

If it cannot answer this question, it should not exist.

## 3 — AI explains

PRIMA never generates without explanation.

Recommendations include:

- pedagogical reasoning  
- expected impact  
- possible alternatives  

Transparency builds trust.

## 4 — Lessons are living designs

A lesson is not a document.  
A lesson is a dynamic learning experience.

**Design → Teach → Reflect → Improve → Repeat.**

## 5 — Simplicity is respect

Teachers should think about teaching, never about software.

Complexity belongs inside the system, not inside the interface.

## 6 — Professional growth is continuous

Every lesson is an opportunity to learn.

Reflection is not bureaucracy.  
Reflection is professional practice.

PRIMA supports lifelong professional development.

## 7 — Learning is human

Technology supports relationships.  
Technology does not replace relationships.

Every feature must strengthen the connection between teacher, student, and learning.

# Our design philosophy

The interface should disappear.

Teachers should focus on learning — not menus, not settings, not configuration.

Calm. Elegant. Intentional. Professional.

---

# Our AI philosophy

Artificial intelligence is invisible.

Teachers experience pedagogy, not algorithms.

AI should intervene only when it provides meaningful value.

**Silence is often better than interruption.**

---

# Our language

PRIMA speaks like an experienced colleague.

Never like a machine.  
Never like a bureaucratic system.  
Never like a technical manual.

Language is clear, respectful, professional, encouraging, and honest.

---

# Our relationship with data

Teachers own their work.  
Teachers control their data.  
Teachers decide what is shared.

Educational data exists only to improve learning.

Never for surveillance.  
Never for unnecessary evaluation.  
Never for commercial exploitation.
