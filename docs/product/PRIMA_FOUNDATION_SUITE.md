# PRIMA Foundation Suite v1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Purpose:**  
Defines the constitutional, strategic, pedagogical, experiential, and engineering foundations of PRIMA.

## Derivation Rule

**Every subsequent document derives from this Foundation Suite.**

- Product definitions, roadmaps, and module briefs derive from DOC-000 · 001 · 002 · 003.  
- Intelligence designs and evaluations derive from DOC-000 · 003 · 004 (and product/UX constraints as applicable).  
- Experience specifications derive from DOC-000 · 002 · 005 (and pedagogy/AI as applicable).  
- Architecture, implementation, and infrastructure decisions derive from DOC-000 · 006 (and all higher educational, product, AI, and UX duties they protect).  

On conflict:

1. **PRIMA-DOC-000** prevails on substance.  
2. Within the Suite, earlier documents constrain later ones.  
3. Lower specifications yield to this Suite on foundation questions.  
4. **PRIMA-DOC-000-AP** prevails on authorship and compliance procedure.
