# LUDI ON — Product Vision

---

## Vision Statement

Become the reference platform for AI-assisted **instructional design** in Romanian schools — where every teacher can design learning experiences as thoughtfully as the best curriculum specialists, in a fraction of the time.

---

## Category Definition

LUDI ON creates a new category:

**AI-Assisted Instructional Design Platform**

Not: lesson generator · not: LMS · not: content library · not: chatbot for teachers.

---

## Ten-Year Horizon

Teachers open LUDI ON the way they open their planner: to **design** what happens in the classroom.

PRIMA knows their classes, respects their style, and co-designs activities, assessments, and resources.

Schools adopt LUDI ON because it improves **instructional quality**, not because it automates paperwork.

---

## Strategic Boundaries

### We will

- Design for pedagogical depth over speed alone
- Keep teachers in control at every decision point
- Build for Romanian curriculum and classroom reality first
- Separate discovery (conversation) from execution (generation)
- Make PRIMA feel human, calm, and trustworthy

### We will not (v1)

- Replace teachers in the classroom
- Auto-publish content to students without teacher review
- Optimize for content volume over learning quality
- Build generic global EdTech — we go deep in Romania first

---

## North Star Metric

A teacher completes a **pedagogically sound, differentiated, classroom-ready activity** in under **5 minutes** — with full understanding of what was created and why.

(Speed sub-metric: configuration under 60 seconds where context is already known.)

## Experience Principles

1. **Dashboard inspires creation** — teachers choose a builder; PRIMA handles design depth
2. **PRIMA is a different world** — emotional entry, no dashboard chrome
3. **Conversation over forms** — discovery feels natural
4. **Blueprint before generation** — pedagogy is explicit and reviewable
5. **Everything is editable** — AI drafts; teachers own the final result

---

## Success in Three Years

- Trusted by Romanian primary teachers as a daily planning partner
- Measurable improvement in self-reported lesson quality and prep time saved
- PRIMA conversation completion rate ≥ 60% for first-time users
- Library of reusable teacher-created activities growing organically
